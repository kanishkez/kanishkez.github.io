SafeSwitch

Tiny Aya refuses 76% of harmful requests in English but only 21% when they're typed in Romanized Hindi or Hinglish. A LoRA adapter trained mostly on Latin-script Hindi fixes most of it without hurting helpfulness or MMLU.

Activation patching shows why: Latin-script prompts never reach the refusal state English builds in layers 14–22.
